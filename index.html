The Firm

A top ranked investor relations firm for the small cap marketplace

Alan Stone & Company, LLC (ASC) is an investor communications, research, consulting and investor relations firm that specializes in developing investor awareness, making professional introductions to the investment community, and enhancing stock market values for emerging growth companies.

We have represented many public companies across a wide range of business sectors. Because of our extensive contacts and long standing relationships within the investment community, our clients have greatly expanded their positioning with institutional investors, brokerage firms, market makers, analysts and individual investors.

Our focus on aggressively communicating each client's achievements and corporate strategy, supported by our own internal research and delivered in a targeted manner, establishes positive sentiment among Wall Street professionals. Working through an extensive network of investors built over a twenty five year career on Wall Street, the firm is a formidable link to the capital markets for emerging publicly traded companies.

After a successful career in New York City, the firm relocated to Los Angeles in 1989 to expand investor relations on the West Coast, and broadened its presence to the Florida investment community in 1999. ASC continues to maintain a significant presence on Wall Street and in Palm Beach.

How We Work

A disciplined program, from first profile to lasting sponsorship

A well structured investor relations program complements both short term and long term business objectives. The investment community wants to know that a company has a plan to meet its stated goals, and that management will back up its words.

By communicating a company's business strategy, combined with research coverage, to our proprietary contact lists, we have arranged numerous successful roadshows and campaigns that achieve enhanced investor consciousness of our clients. The result is increased or stabilized valuations that a company can then use as a tool for financing or acquisitions.

What the program is designed to achieve

  • Increase brokerage community awareness
  • Add to the number of active retail brokers
  • Draw the attention of research analysts
  • Enhance media coverage
  • Attract institutional interest
  • Attain greater liquidity
  • Broaden the shareholder base
  • Attract quality market makers
  • Raise sentiment among potential investors
  • Provide focused investor intelligence
  • Arrange roadshows to enhance visibility
  • Ensure the stock price better reflects performance

  1. Corporate profile or research report

    The strategy starts with preparation of a corporate profile or an institutional quality research report on the client company, covering strategy, competitive advantages, financial condition, management and outlook.

  2. Targeted distribution

    A direct mail and email campaign is set in motion shortly thereafter. The profile or report goes to our contacts and to a proprietary database of professional and individual investors, so a large audience quickly learns the client's results, potential and direction.

  3. Personal outreach

    Concurrently, we launch an active phone campaign to personally highlight the essential and attractive features of the client company to the key brokers, institutions and investment banking firms we have selected.

  4. Meetings and roadshows

    We carefully select the institutions, brokers and analysts most likely to be interested, then arrange meetings between management and those firms in leading financial centers.

  5. Ongoing sponsorship

    On a regular basis we distribute news releases, analyst and newsletter research, summary quarterly financial data and profile updates. We assist with press releases, stockholder reports and annual reports, and can arrange printing, design, corporate video and slide presentations at economical prices.

  6. Access to capital and exchange listings

    We help emerging companies list on more active trading markets, and introduce management to underwriters and financing sources as the program builds momentum.
